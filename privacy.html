Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short

No accounts, no sign-up, and we don’t sell your data. Most of what the Site remembers stays on your device.

Stored on your device

Your theme and settings, game scores, custom games, whether you accepted the Terms, and an AI key if you chose to save one are kept in your browser’s local storage. We can’t see them. Clear them with Settings → Reset or by clearing your browser data.

What our hosting sees

The Site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser under its own privacy statement. We don’t run a server that stores visitor data.

Games and ads

When you open a game it loads from a third party (such as Playgama or an independent developer). They may collect your IP address, device information, cookies, advertising identifiers and gameplay data under their own policies. We take part in Playgama’s partner program, so plays of its games are linked to our partner ID and we may earn revenue from them.

Fonts and images

After you accept the Terms, the Site loads fonts from Google Fonts and game images from Playgama’s servers, which receive your IP address when you do. Before you accept, none of these are requested.

AI assistant

Messages you type are sent to Groq to generate a reply, through a relay run by the site owner. We don’t store them. The conversation exists only in the page’s memory and disappears when you reload. Please don’t share personal or sensitive information.

Children

NullDen is not for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has shared personal information, contact us and we’ll help.

Your choices

You can decline the Terms and leave, clear stored data at any time, block cookies, or use your browser’s privacy tools.
