Security

Last updated: October 23, 2026

How the Site is built

  • The Site is served over HTTPS, and we never ask for payment details or email addresses.
  • If you make an account, your password is stored only as a salted hash and sign-in sessions are signed tokens. Nullbux balances and items are kept on the server, not in your browser.
  • Embedded games run in sandboxed frames with limited permissions.
  • Settings stay in your own browser.
  • Moderation tools are limited to the site owner and checked on the server every time.

What we can’t control

Games, ads and links come from third parties. Never enter passwords, payment details or personal information into a game, and don’t download files or install anything a game asks you to. Leave any page that seems suspicious.

Staying safe

  • Don’t share personal information with games.
  • Don’t reuse passwords from your real accounts anywhere on the Site.
  • Keep your browser up to date.

Reporting a problem

If you find a security issue or harmful content, email zzearyd@gmail.com with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please don’t access other people’s data or disrupt the Site while testing. We’ll look into reports as quickly as we can.

No guarantee

No website is perfectly secure, and we can’t promise third-party content is safe.
