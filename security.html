Security

Last updated: October 23, 2026

How the Site is built

  • It is a static site served over HTTPS. There are no accounts, passwords or payment details to steal.
  • Embedded games run in sandboxed frames with limited permissions.
  • The AI service key is kept on a relay server and is never placed in the page.
  • Settings stay in your own browser.

What we can’t control

Games, ads and links come from third parties. Never enter passwords, payment details or personal information into a game, and don’t download files or install anything a game asks you to. Leave any page that seems suspicious.

Staying safe

  • Don’t share personal information with games or the AI assistant.
  • Don’t reuse passwords from your real accounts anywhere on the Site.
  • Keep your browser up to date.

Reporting a problem

If you find a security issue or harmful content, email [add your contact email here] with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please don’t access other people’s data or disrupt the Site while testing. We’ll look into reports as quickly as we can.

No guarantee

No website is perfectly secure, and we can’t promise third-party content is safe.
